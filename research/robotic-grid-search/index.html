Research/Robotic grid-search platform

A robotic platform for mapping copolymer phase behavior

Hundreds of thin-film samples fabricated in hours, measured by synchrotron scattering, and turned into the first high-throughput map of the order–disorder transition landscape in triblock copolymer–homopolymer blends.

Headline result

Throughput first

A closed-loop optimizer is only as good as the throughput underneath it. I led development of the polymer processing robots that fabricate hundreds of thin-film samples within hours — and, just as important, make them the same way every time, so variation across a library reflects the composition rather than the hand.

Mapping the order–disorder transition

The target was the phase behavior of ABA triblock copolymer–homopolymer blends. The order–disorder boundary is easy to step over with a coarse grid; robotic fabrication feeding SAXS/WAXS at the ALS and NSLS-II, with high-throughput AFM for real-space confirmation, sampled it densely enough to resolve it. A reduction pipeline built on Bluesky and Tiled kept pace with fabrication.

Six chemistries, one database

PS-P2VPPS-PEOPI-PS-P2VPPS-P4VPPB-PS-PBPI-PS-PI

The data is published openly at polymermorphology.org and became the training set for the machine learning framework and the groundwork for the closed-loop work.
